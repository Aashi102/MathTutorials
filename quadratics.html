Quadratics

Work with parabolas and quadratic equations.

What Is a Quadratic?

A quadratic is an equation with a highest power of 2, usually written ax^2 + bx + c = 0.

Factoring to Solve

Solve x^2 + 5x + 6 = 0.

Factor: (x + 2)(x + 3) = 0 → x = -2 or x = -3.

Vertex Form

y = a(x - h)^2 + k has vertex at (h, k).

Find the vertex of y = (x - 1)^2 + 4.

Vertex: (1, 4).

Practice

  1. Identify if 3x^2 - x + 2 is quadratic.
  2. Solve x^2 - 9 = 0.
  3. Factor x^2 + 7x + 10.
  4. Find the vertex of y = (x + 2)^2 - 3.
