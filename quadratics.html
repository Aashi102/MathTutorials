Quadratics

Explore parabolas, roots, and the quadratic formula.

Standard Form

A quadratic is written as ax² + bx + c.

Vertex

The vertex is the highest or lowest point of the parabola.

Example: y = (x − 2)² + 3

Vertex: (2, 3)

Quadratic Formula

x = (−b ± √(b² − 4ac)) / 2a

Example: Solve x² − 5x + 6 = 0

Roots: 2 and 3
