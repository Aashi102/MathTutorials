Inequalities

Learn how to solve, manipulate, and graph inequalities.

What Is an Inequality?

An inequality compares two values using symbols:

  • < — less than
  • > — greater than
  • ≤ — less than or equal to
  • ≥ — greater than or equal to

Example: x + 3 > 7

Solving Inequalities

Solve inequalities just like equations — with one important exception.

If you multiply or divide by a negative number, flip the inequality sign.

Solve x - 5 < 2.

Add 5 → x < 7

Inequalities with Negatives

Solve -3x ≥ 12.

Divide by -3 → flip sign → x ≤ -4

Graphing Solutions

Use:

  • Open circle for < or >
  • Closed circle for ≤ or ≥

x ≥ 2 → closed circle at 2, shade right

Practice

  1. Solve x + 4 > 9.
  2. Solve 2x ≤ 10.
  3. Solve -5x > 20.
  4. Graph the solution to x < -1.
