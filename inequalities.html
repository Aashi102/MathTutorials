Solving Inequalities

Inequalities compare values and often have many possible solutions. They help us describe ranges, limits, and conditions in real‑world situations.

What Is an Inequality?

An inequality compares two values using symbols like:

  • < (less than)
  • > (greater than)
  • ≤ (less than or equal to)
  • ≥ (greater than or equal to)

Example: x > 4 means x can be 5, 6, 7…

Solving Inequalities

Solving inequalities is similar to solving equations — except for one important rule.

Example: Solve x + 3 > 7

Subtract 3 → x > 4.

Important Rule: Flipping the Sign

If you multiply or divide both sides by a negative number, the inequality sign flips direction.

Example: Solve −2x ≤ 10

Divide by −2 → x ≥ −5.

Practice Problems

  • Solve: x − 2 < 5
  • Solve: 3x ≥ 12
  • Solve: −4y > 20
  • Solve: 2x + 1 ≤ 9
