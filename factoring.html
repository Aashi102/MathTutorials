Factoring Techniques

Factoring breaks expressions into simpler pieces. It’s the reverse of multiplying and is essential for solving many equations.

Greatest Common Factor (GCF)

The GCF is the largest value that divides all terms.

Example: Factor 6x + 12

GCF = 6 → 6(x + 2)

Factoring Trinomials

Look for two numbers that multiply to c and add to b.

Example: x² + 5x + 6

Numbers: 2 and 3 → (x + 2)(x + 3)
