Factoring

Rewrite expressions as products of simpler factors.

Greatest Common Factor (GCF)

Factor out the largest common factor of all terms.

Factor 6x + 9.

GCF is 3 → 3(2x + 3)

Factoring Trinomials

Look for two numbers that multiply to ac and add to b.

Factor x^2 + 5x + 6.

(x + 2)(x + 3)

Difference of Squares

Pattern: a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)

Factor x^2 - 16.

(x - 4)(x + 4)
