📌 Translating Words to Algebra

Many problems describe relationships that can be written as equations.

Example

“A number increased by 7 is 20.”

Equation: x + 7 = 20 → x = 13

🧮 Rate Problems

Example

A car travels 60 miles per hour. How far in 3 hours?

Distance = rate × time → 180 miles
