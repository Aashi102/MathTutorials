Algebra Word Problems

Translate real‑world situations into algebraic expressions and equations.

Step 1: Identify the Unknown

Decide what the variable represents.

Example: “Sarah has 5 more apples than Tom.”

Let x = Tom’s apples → Sarah = x + 5

Step 2: Write an Equation

Use relationships in the problem to form an equation.

Example: “Together they have 17 apples.”

x + (x + 5) = 17

Step 3: Solve & Interpret

Always interpret your solution in the context of the problem.

Example: Solve the equation above.

x = 6 → Tom has 6 apples, Sarah has 11.
