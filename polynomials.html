📌 What Is a Polynomial?

A polynomial is an expression made of terms with variables raised to whole-number exponents.

Examples

✔ 3x² + 2x - 7

✔ 5x³ - x

🧮 Adding & Subtracting Polynomials

Combine like terms.

Example

(3x + 5) + (2x - 1)

→ 5x + 4

✖ Multiplying Polynomials (FOIL)

Example

(x + 3)(x + 2)

→ x² + 5x + 6
