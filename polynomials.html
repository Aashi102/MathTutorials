Polynomials

Polynomials are expressions with multiple terms. They form the foundation for factoring, quadratics, and more advanced algebra.

What Is a Polynomial?

A polynomial is a sum of terms, each made of a coefficient, a variable, and an exponent.

Example: 3x² − 5x + 2

Adding Polynomials

Combine like terms — terms with the same variable and exponent.

Example: (3x + 2) + (5x − 1)

3x + 5x = 8x 2 − 1 = 1 Final: 8x + 1

Multiplying Polynomials

Use distribution or FOIL (First, Outer, Inner, Last).

Example: (x + 3)(x + 2)

x² + 2x + 3x + 6 = x² + 5x + 6
