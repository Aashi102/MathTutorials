Polynomials

Work with expressions that have multiple terms and powers.

What Is a Polynomial?

A polynomial is a sum of terms of the form ax^n, where n is a whole number.

Example: 3x^2 + 2x - 5

Degree and Terms

The degree is the highest exponent. Each part separated by + or - is a term.

Find the degree of 4x^3 - x + 7.

Highest exponent is 3 → degree 3.

Adding and Subtracting Polynomials

Combine like terms.

(3x^2 + 2x) + (x^2 - 5x) = 4x^2 - 3x

Multiplying Polynomials

Use distribution or FOIL for binomials.

Multiply (x + 2)(x + 3).

x^2 + 5x + 6

Practice

  1. Identify the degree of 2x^4 - x^2 + 7.
  2. Add (3x^2 + x) and (x^2 - 4x).
  3. Subtract (5x^2 - 3) from (7x^2 + 1).
  4. Multiply (x - 1)(x + 4).
