Graphing

Work with the coordinate plane and graph linear equations.

The Coordinate Plane

The coordinate plane has an x-axis (horizontal) and y-axis (vertical). A point is written as (x, y).

Example: (3, -2) is 3 units right, 2 units down.

Plotting Points

Describe where the point (-4, 1) is located.

4 units left, 1 unit up from the origin.

Graphing Linear Equations

A linear equation can be written as y = mx + b, where m is the slope and b is the y-intercept.

Example: y = 2x + 1 has slope 2 and y-intercept 1.

What is the slope and y-intercept of y = -3x + 4?

Slope: -3, y-intercept: 4.

Practice

  1. Plot the point (2, 5).
  2. Identify the coordinates of a point 3 left and 4 down from the origin.
  3. Find the slope and intercept of y = x - 2.
  4. Graph y = -2x + 3.
