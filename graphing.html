📌 Slope

The slope tells you how steep a line is. Formula:

m = rise / run

Example

Points: (2, 3) and (5, 9)

m = (9 − 3) / (5 − 2) = 6 / 3 = 2

📌 Slope-Intercept Form

y = mx + b

  • m = slope
  • b = y-intercept

Example

y = 2x + 1 → slope = 2, intercept = 1
