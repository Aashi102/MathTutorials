📌 What Is an Equation?

An equation states that two expressions are equal. Your goal is to find the value of the variable.

Example

Solve: x + 5 = 12

Subtract 5 → x = 7

🧮 Two-Step Equations

Example

Solve: 3x - 4 = 11

Add 4 → 3x = 15

Divide by 3 → x = 5
