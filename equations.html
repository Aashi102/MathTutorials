Equations

Learn how to solve one-step, two-step, and multi-step equations.

What Is an Equation?

An equation states that two expressions are equal. Your goal is to find the value of the variable that makes the equation true.

3x + 4 = 19

One-Step Equations

Undo the operation to isolate the variable.

x + 7 = 12 → subtract 7 → x = 5

Solve x - 9 = 3.

Add 9 to both sides → x = 12

Two-Step Equations

Undo addition/subtraction first, then multiplication/division.

2x + 3 = 11 Subtract 3 → 2x = 8 Divide by 2 → x = 4

Solve 3x - 5 = 16.

Add 5 → 3x = 21
Divide by 3 → x = 7

Equations with Distribution

2(x + 3) = 18

Solve 4(x - 2) = 20.

Distribute: 4x - 8 = 20
Add 8 → 4x = 28
Divide by 4 → x = 7

Practice

  4. Solve 2(x + 5) = 26.
