Solving Equations

Equations are statements that two expressions are equal. Solving an equation means finding the value of the variable that makes the statement true.

What Is an Equation?

An equation contains an equals sign (=). It tells us that two expressions have the same value.

Examples:

  • x + 5 = 12
  • 3y − 2 = 10
  • 2(a + 1) = 14

One‑Step Equations

Use inverse operations to isolate the variable. If the equation adds something, subtract it. If it multiplies, divide.

Example: Solve x + 7 = 12

Subtract 7 from both sides → x = 5.

Two‑Step Equations

Undo addition/subtraction first, then multiplication/division.

Example: Solve 2x − 4 = 10

Add 4 → 2x = 14 → divide by 2 → x = 7.

Practice Problems

  • Solve: x − 3 = 9
  • Solve: 5x = 20
  • Solve: 3x + 2 = 17
  • Solve: 4y − 6 = 10
