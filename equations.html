Solving Equations

Learn how to isolate variables and solve linear equations.

What is an Equation?

An equation states that two expressions are equal.

It contains an equals sign (=).

Solving One‑Step Equations

Use inverse operations to isolate the variable.

Example: Solve x + 7 = 12

Subtract 7 → x = 5

Two‑Step Equations

Undo addition/subtraction first, then multiplication/division.

Example: Solve 2x − 4 = 10

Add 4 → 2x = 14 → divide by 2 → x = 7
