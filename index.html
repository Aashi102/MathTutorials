Why Algebra Matters

Algebra is the foundation of logical thinking. It teaches you how to break down problems, recognize patterns, and build relationships between quantities. These skills show up everywhere — science, programming, finance, engineering, and everyday decisions.

Example:

A phone plan charges a base fee plus a cost per gigabyte. That’s an algebraic relationship. When you write it as an equation, you can compare plans and predict costs.
