Why Algebra Matters

Algebra is the foundation of logical reasoning. It helps you break down problems, recognize patterns, and connect quantities — skills that power science, technology, and everyday decisions.

Example:

A phone plan charges a base fee plus a cost per gigabyte. Write it as y = 10 + 5x — now you can predict costs and compare plans instantly.
