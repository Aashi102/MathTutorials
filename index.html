Why Algebra Matters

Algebra builds logical thinking. It teaches you how to break down problems, recognize patterns, and understand relationships between quantities.

Example:

A phone plan charges a base fee plus a cost per gigabyte. That’s an algebraic relationship — write it as an equation, and you can compare plans and predict costs.
